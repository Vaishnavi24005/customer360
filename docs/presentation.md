# Ten-minute presentation guide

| Slide | Time | Speaker guide |
|---|---:|---|
| Customer 360 | 0:45 | Set up the business question. Marketing sees channel activity and CRM sees customers. A shared customer view lets the team explain which touches precede each order. |
| Data and identity | 1:20 | Explain how the BigQuery path combines GA4 with simulated app and CRM records. Show how one app ID and one web ID map to one CRM ID. Describe how an ambiguous assertion stays anonymous and how a repeated transaction is deduplicated. |
| Customer segments | 1:15 | Define recency, frequency and monetary value. Explain why the 24 Champions and 54 At risk profiles would receive different retention treatments. Show how the gross-margin-based annualized proxy scales observed activity. |
| Attribution comparison | 1:40 | Define the 30-day eligibility window. Walk through four models, then highlight the $22,492 to $1,970 Paid Social swing. Open the demo and switch between First touch and Last touch. |
| Campaign economics | 1:20 | Explain the 55% margin assumption, credited acquisition denominator and blank ratios for unknown cost. Compare Email, Brand Search and Paid Social under Time decay. |
| Validation and limitations | 1:15 | Show the order-level reconciliation and grain checks. Explain where the offline fixture begins and why a warehouse test and real cost data are needed. |
| Budget experiment | 1:30 | Describe the $450 reallocation in the simulation. Explain the holdout design, incremental gross profit metric and guardrails on acquisition and unsubscribes. Preserve Paid Social prospecting during the test. |
| Next steps | 0:55 | Cover the warehouse run, verified identity and actual costs. Describe who would own the experiment and how its result would inform the budget. |

Total speaking time is 10:00. Figures describe the synthetic offline run.
